# Magnusim 0.5.0: release notes (draft)

The Assistant: a model you choose (a cloud provider with your own key, a local server on this
PC, or a coding agent you already use) works in Magnusim through the app's own tools, asking
before anything that starts a computation or can't be undone. It goes into WHATS-NEW.txt when
0.5.0 is released.

## The Assistant outside CAD

- From Home, the workbench and a CAD Part, the Assistant can do the whole modelling chain: list
  and create projects, import a geometry you attach in its panel, read faces and bodies, set up a
  study with materials and boundary conditions, mesh, run, and read the results as numbers.
- It moves your window as your own clicks do (open a project, a study, a panel, a CAD Part, and
  Back, which sends a CAD design to the simulation), and takes pictures of the view (on Home, a
  drawing of the window: the project cards as you see them).
- One question can cover "mesh, then run": the card names both jobs, and nothing else.
- What it changes shows at once in the open window; a panel you were editing says when the
  Assistant changed the same thing, and keeps the saved values.
- Meshing now has a Stop, in the Mesh panel and the tree.

## A tidier window

- Home's header is **Settings** and **New project** only. New project is where you choose what a
  project starts with: geometry to import, a design (CAD Part) or a 2D wind tunnel; picking one
  fills in its title, so one Create click makes it. A folder comes from the right-click menu on
  My Projects. Open sample is gone from Home (the Assistant still opens a sample when asked).
- The Assistant has no header button: a small **Assistant** button sits in the bottom-right
  corner whenever its panel is closed (it says what is going on while a request runs), and the
  panel starts closed when the app opens. Its settings are in Settings → Assistant.

## Not yet in v0.5.0

These come in v0.5.x. Until then you do them yourself; the Assistant says so when asked.

- Results view (v0.5.x): the field and legend range, cut planes, parts colouring and regions,
  particle traces, iso surfaces and iso volumes, animation, compare two meshes or results, record
  a video, and open a plugin case's results in 3D. The Assistant can already read the numbers
  (field ranges, point and line values, monitors) and take a picture of what you see.
- Saved views (v0.5.x): save or update a saved view (it captures the live camera and filters).
  Renaming and deleting one work in 0.5.0.
- Pictures and recordings (v0.5.x): rename a picture or recording, and open the case folder.
- Settings (v0.5.x): preferred units, the local port, collapsing finished sections, turning
  plugins on or off, reloading plugins, and a plugin's own preferences. The Assistant's own
  settings stay yours alone.
- The debug drawer (v0.5.x): attach a results case by path.
- Plugins' own tools (v0.5.x): the Airfoil and 2D Wind Tunnel workspaces don't offer the
  Assistant tools yet, so it can't import a .dat file into them or run them.
